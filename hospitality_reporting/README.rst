Hospitality Management - Reporting
==================================
* This module provides reporting and analytics for the Hospitality Management
  suite. It enables daily hospitality KPI tracking, manager flash reports,
  daily revenue summaries, and operational performance monitoring across
  properties. It also includes interactive reporting dashboards, scheduled KPI
  generation, property-based reporting, and printable reports to support
  management decision-making.
