Hospitality Management - Rate
=============================
* This module provides rate and pricing management for the Hospitality
  Management suite. It enables the creation of rate plans, rate codes,
  seasonal pricing, occupancy-based pricing, restriction management,
  packages and inclusions, distribution channels, and rate audits.
  It also includes an interactive rate calendar, restrictions matrix,
  weekday mask widgets, and scheduled processing for hospitality pricing.
